About PublicSpace360

“Public Space Usage and Digital Mapping” — A Community Engagement Project for Urban Infrastructure Optimization

Project Vision

Transforming Civic Spaces Through Citizen Data

Public urban spaces—parks, waterfronts, community gardens, and open-air athletic gyms—form the social and ecological backbone of modern smart cities. However, many municipalities lack high-granularity, crowd-sourced digital maps indicating how different age groups utilize these facilities and where maintenance deficits arise.

PublicSpace360 bridges this crucial gap between citizens and urban planners. By combining interactive GIS mapping (Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap) with rigorous citizen survey telemetry and real-time maintenance ticketing, the platform delivers actionable empirical insights that inform municipal resource allocation.

Project Methodology & Architecture

Field Audit & GIS Georeferencing

Auditing metropolitan parks, promenades, and gardens, recording coordinates, operational hours, conditions, and amenities.

Citizen Community Surveying

Deploying a structured 12-question metric assessing perceived safety, illumination, water potability, and universal accessibility.

Empirical Visualization & Telemetry

Aggregating responses using Flask REST APIs and Chart.js dynamic telemetry charts for smart city administrators.

Civic Issue Resolution Workflow

Allowing citizens to report broken infrastructure with photo verification, trackable from "Reported" to "Resolved".
